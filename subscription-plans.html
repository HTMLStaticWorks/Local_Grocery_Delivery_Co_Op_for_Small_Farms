Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pause my subscription for vacation?

Yes! You can easily pause your deliveries through your Subscriber Dashboard up to 48 hours before your next scheduled delivery.

How do customizations (swaps) work?

Each Friday, we announce the upcoming week's harvest. You can log into your dashboard and swap out items you don't want for other available produce, up to your plan's limit.

What is the delivery area?

We currently deliver within a 25-mile radius of the city center. You can check your eligibility by entering your zip code during registration.

Still deciding?

Not sure which box fits?

Tell us about your household and we'll help you pick the right plan. No contracts, pause or cancel anytime.
