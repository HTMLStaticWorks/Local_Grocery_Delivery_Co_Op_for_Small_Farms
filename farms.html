Meet the growers

The families behind your box

Four independent farms, all within 25 miles, each bringing their own specialty to your weekly harvest.

Green Valley Acres

12 miles away • Springfield

Founded by the Thompson family in 2010, Green Valley Acres transformed a depleted plot into a thriving, regenerative ecosystem. They specialize in heirloom tomatoes, root vegetables, and seasonal greens, all grown without synthetic pesticides.

Sunrise Orchard

18 miles away • Westbridge

A fourth-generation family orchard providing crisp apples, pears, and summer berries. They practice integrated pest management and use natural predators to keep the fruit healthy and safe.

Urban Roots Co.

5 miles away • City Center

Pioneering sustainable urban farming in repurposed spaces. Urban Roots supplies our boxes with nutrient-dense microgreens, specialty herbs, and gourmet mushrooms.

Riverbend Creamery

24 miles away • Easton

A small-herd, pasture-raised dairy providing artisanal cheeses, grass-fed butter, and fresh milk. Animal welfare is their top priority.
